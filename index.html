A Safe, Joyful Foundation for Your Child

Run under Belaku Education Trust (R), Sri Sai Blossom Kids in Vidyuth Nagar, Hassan is committed to providing an affectionate, secure, and inspiring early childhood environment.

We understand that the first years of learning shape a child's confidence, curiosity, and character. Guided by our motto “Rise to the Top”, our dedicated educators nurture every little step with personal care, healthy routines, and engaging foundational activities.

🎒 PROGRAMS & SERVICES

Services Offered At Sri Sai Blossom Kids

Clear, age-tailored programs designed to support your child's developmental milestones and family needs.

🏡 Service 01
Day Care

Day Care

A warm, safe, and attentive home-away-from-home for young children. Provides personalized care, healthy rest schedules, hygienic play spaces, and gentle supervision while parents are at work.

  • ✓ Clean, safe & comfortable environment
  • ✓ Guided play & structured rest routines
  • ✓ Attentive, loving caregivers
🧸 Service 02
Baby Sitting

Baby Sitting

Affectionate, individualized attention for our littlest arrivals. Ensuring complete peace of mind for families with vigilant guardianship, sensory engagement, and tender care.

  • ✓ Individual attention for toddlers
  • ✓ Tactile & sensory toy engagement
  • ✓ Strict hygiene & safety protocols
✏️ Service 03
L.K.G.

L.K.G. (Lower Kindergarten)

Building early language fluency, alphabet recognition, number concepts, and motor dexterity. Through singing rhymes, finger painting, and group games, learning becomes an exciting daily adventure.

  • ✓ Phonics, rhymes & storytelling circles
  • ✓ Number sense, shapes & patterns
  • ✓ Social skills & cooperative games
🎓 Service 04
U.K.G.

U.K.G. (Upper Kindergarten)

Preparing confident young minds for primary school success. Fosters pre-reading fluency, structured handwriting, basic arithmetic, environmental awareness, and independent thinking.

  • ✓ Pre-reading fluency & handwriting
  • ✓ Foundational math & science concepts
  • ✓ Primary school readiness & confidence

Environment Day Celebration

Instilling care for nature and the environment right from early childhood.
